// 01 about

I am a researcher with strong interests in swarm robotics, autonomous agents, deep reinforcement learning, and intelligent robotic systems. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from North South University.

Currently a Research Assistant at the NSU Intelligent Robotics (NIRO) Laboratory, my work focuses on swarm robotics architectures — integrating machine learning into both centralized and decentralized control systems to improve swarm coordination, adaptability, and efficiency.

I am actively seeking collaborative research opportunities and exploring graduate-level research (MS/PhD) to advance intelligent robotics and autonomous systems.

// 02 research interests

  • Swarm intelligence — centralized & decentralized control architectures for robot collectives
  • AI-driven robotic systems — ML-enhanced coordination, adaptability, and decision-making
  • Computer vision — perception for autonomous agents, segmentation-based vision for DRL
  • Motion planning & navigation — autonomous exploration, obstacle avoidance, CAD-based robotic design
