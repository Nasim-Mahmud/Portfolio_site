// 01 about

I am a researcher with strong interests in swarm robotics, autonomous agents, deep reinforcement learning, and intelligent robotic systems. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from North South University.

At the NSU Intelligent Robotics (NIRO) Laboratory I work on swarm robotics, integrating machine learning into centralized and decentralized control systems to improve coordination, adaptability, and efficiency. Before that I was a Part-time Research Assistant in the Dept. of ECE at NSU, working on resource-efficient visual deep reinforcement learning for autonomous robots, with an emphasis on segmentation-based vision and custom datasets.

I am also a founding member of NSU Ignite, a robotics team, where I helped design and build robots for competitions and ran hands-on workshops for new members.

I am open to research collaborations and graduate-level research (MS/PhD) in robotics and autonomous systems.
